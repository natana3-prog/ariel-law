Accessibility Statement

The Ariel Amar Law Office considers it of utmost importance to make its services accessible to the entire public, including people with disabilities, and operates in accordance with the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Accessibility Adjustments to Service) Regulations, 5773-2013.

This website was built in accordance with Israeli Standard 5568 at the AA level, based on the W3C's WCAG 2.0 guidelines.

An accessibility component is installed on the site, accessible via the fixed icon on the side of the screen, allowing: increasing/decreasing text size, high contrast, inverted colors, grayscale, highlighted links, readable font and resetting settings.

Additional adjustments on the site include: semantic heading structure, full keyboard navigation, alt text for images, proper color contrast and screen reader support.

Some parts or content may not yet be fully accessible — we are continuously working to fix this.
