What is the difference between a will and an inheritance order?

A will is a document a person writes during their lifetime determining how their assets will be divided after their death. An inheritance order is issued by the court when a person passes away without a will, and the assets are divided according to the Inheritance Law.

What is an enduring power of attorney and who is it suitable for?

An enduring power of attorney is a legal document that allows a person to appoint an attorney-in-fact to handle their affairs (medical, financial and personal) if they reach a state in which they cannot make decisions themselves. It is suitable for any adult who wishes to plan for the future.

How long does a real estate transaction take?

A typical real estate transaction takes between 3 and 6 months from signing the agreement to registration of rights. The duration depends on the type of transaction, its complexity and external factors such as authorities and banks.

Can a will be contested?

Yes, it is possible to file an objection to a will on various grounds such as: lack of capacity of the testator, undue influence, formal defects and more. It is important to contact an attorney specializing in inheritance as soon as possible.
